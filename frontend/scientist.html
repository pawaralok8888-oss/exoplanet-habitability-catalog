CATALOG CONTEXT

How to read these estimates

Summary of the connected catalog.

Exploratory ranking

The refreshed model test set contains only 11 positive examples. Its high accuracy is not enough to establish reliable ranking or calibration.

Class bands are display filters: High ≥ 0.70, Medium 0.40–0.69, Low < 0.40. They are not validated scientific cutoffs.

Catalog measurements and derived values should be interpreted with their source and completeness. Model estimates are not evidence of life. Use “Export matching records” to download the current filtered catalog.
